Features

  • Curated collection

    Ten hand-picked photographs that capture the energy, intimacy and joy of making music — arranged in a modern, adaptive grid.

  • Responsive by design

    Built mobile-first with Flexbox, CSS Grid and fluid typography, so every layout feels intentional from 320px phones to ultrawide displays.

  • Immersive viewing

    Open any image in a full-screen, keyboard-friendly viewer with swipe support and optimised AVIF and WebP delivery.

About

Hello, and welcome

This project started as a small experiment with Flexbox and has grown into a showcase of modern front-end craft: semantic HTML, a token-based design system and carefully tuned interactions.

Every photograph tells a story about music — the concentration of a lone guitarist in the dark, the unfiltered happiness of a performer on stage, or the quiet calm of an instrument resting in morning light.

Take your time, scroll through the collection and open any image to see it up close.
